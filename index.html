TripKit · Smart Travel Upcoming Product

In-Flight Telemetry & Offline Travel Suite

TripKit is the all-in-one flight and travel companion built with Flutter. Keep track of live GNSS flight telemetry even in airplane mode, organize dynamic day-by-day itineraries, and play multiplayer lounge games completely offline.

✓

In-Flight Satellite Telemetry: Real-time altitude, ground speed, and route ETA computed on-device with zero Wi-Fi.

✓

Offline Nearby Lounge: Multiplayer P2P Rummikub and Chess using Bluetooth & Multipeer connectivity.

✓

Digital Travel Wallet & eSIM: Stacked boarding passes with QR codes, airport terminal gate maps, and eSIM data.
